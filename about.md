# Sort By

**Sort By** is a simple, lightweight mod that adds a custom "Sort" button directly to the "My Levels" screen. 

### Features
Instantly reorganize your created levels based on:
* **Alphabetical Order:** Sorts your levels from A to Z.
* **Length:** Sorts from longest to shortest (Platformer/XL down to Tiny).
* **Time Spent:** Sorts by the levels you have spent the most time building in the editor.

### Compatibility
* Fully compatible with Geometry Dash 2.206+

### Dependencies
* **Node IDs** version >=1.20.0 is required.
